# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **Better Companions (new):** an adventurer from one of your other save slots (`/companion 2`; your slot is only
  read). It follows you and fights with its own gear, level and XP; middle click sends it; enemies fight it back; at
  zero health hold F to revive it. F at it opens the inventory with its gear on top: dress it from your bag, and
  spend its attribute points on the character panel. Swaps between a melee and a ranged weapon and dashes in and out
  of fights. Everyone sees everyone's companion in co-op.
- **Co-op story gates:** a cutscene's gates (Adeos' entrance gate, the HummingSphere gate) follow the player who set
  the cutscene off, so they open for teammates when that player's fight is done.

## 0.3.2
- **Chase and first-person views (experimental):** `/chase` and `/fps` (or Insert > View): the camera behind you or
  through your eyes, turned by the mouse (Alt frees the cursor), W walks the way you face, the walls raised as blocks.
- **Tilt-shift:** `/tiltshift`: the miniature look (a sharp middle band, blurred top and bottom, richer colours).
- **Tilted view:** houses stand whole (vines and windows stay on them, trees behind sort behind the roof, the world's
  edge no longer cuts their tops); stairs and rugs stay lying. Reset buttons on the sliders.
- **Partial controller support:** lock-on on a controller: hold the right trigger to lock on to the nearest enemy (hold
  again: let go), tap it for the next one. Locked, you face the enemy whatever the right stick does, so arrows and
  spells go at it.
- **Lock-on fixes:** G works in the dungeons (it was ignored there), and the lock ring shows on the dungeon floor.

## 0.3.1
- **Tilted view (experimental):** `/tilt` or Insert > View: the world from behind and above in perspective, everything
  that stands stood up facing you. Only the picture changes; your aim, mouse walking, pings and the Hunter's Hook follow
  what you see. Tilt and field of view sliders.

## 0.3.0
- **Lock-on (G):** point at an enemy and press G to lock on: you face it, and swings, spells and arrows go at it. G
  again lets go; G at nothing locks the nearest enemy, then the next. Enemies only, never doors or furniture.
- **Shared stash (U, `/stash`):** 6 tabs of 30 slots shared by every character on your PC; rename and colour the tabs.
- **Ctrl+click** moves an item between your bag and an open chest.
- **Font Options** (Insert menu): story text size, the size of labels in the world ("Open", "Examine", names), and the
  font (the game's Emberfont, Noto Serif, Liberation Sans, Perfect DOS VGA).
- Staff tooltips no longer overlap the second item card; renamed chests show their name straight away.

## 0.2.0
- Town portal from anywhere, `/tp` and `/summon`, coloured portals per player.
- Better Weather: rain storms with lightning, snow that settles across the map, melts and takes footprints.
- The dungeon's floor rings sit over walls and props; weather is shared when you join.

## 0.1.x
- Online co-op (host by IP or through Steam friends), our own random dungeon (the descent), the boss rush arena,
  favourite weapons and the weapon wheel, Better Chests, Better Looting, Better Pets and Summons (the Staff of Clucking,
  the Staff of the Risen), Better Fishing, Better NPCs and Quests (Ellie), the Hunter's Hook, gear dyes.
